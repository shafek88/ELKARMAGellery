Introduction

ELKARMAGallery ("we", "our", "the website") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use elkarmagallery.com, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

Your Photos and Content

When you upload photos to create a slideshow, they are processed and stored locally in your own browser (using your browser's local storage) — they are not uploaded to our servers and we do not have access to them. Your photos remain on your device only, for the duration of your browser session, and are removed when you clear your browser data or use the "Clear" option on the site.

Information We Collect

We may automatically collect limited technical information such as device type, browser type, approximate location (city/country level), and pages visited, in order to understand usage and improve the website. We also collect any information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, or message, if you use the contact form or feedback form.

Contact Form and Feedback

The contact form on this site is processed using EmailJS, a third-party service that delivers your message to us by email. Feedback submitted through the feedback form is stored locally in your browser and is not automatically shared with third parties. We do not sell or rent any information you provide.

Cookies and Local Storage

This website uses cookies and browser local storage to remember your display settings (such as chosen effect and duration) and to enable offline functionality through a service worker. You can disable cookies or clear local storage at any time in your browser settings; doing so may reset your saved preferences.

These partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data and opt out of personalized advertising at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-Party Links

This website contains links to third-party platforms, including Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal information we hold about you. Since most content you create stays in your browser and is never sent to us, there is generally nothing on our servers to delete. For any data-related request, contact us using the details below.
